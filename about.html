Who We Are

Project Overview

Sunflower Park is a mixed development (residential and commercial) housing project with adequate provision for green spaces security and power.

It is located behind T.A Gardens Estate, on the very fast developing Lagos-Ibadan expressway. The park is less than 25 minutes drive from the Lagos state government secretariat, Alausa, Ikeja and is bordered by; Wisdom Cooperative Estate, Forthright Estate, Eden Park Estate and Regeny Estates to mention a few.

We also have affordable and durable ecofriendly housing options.
