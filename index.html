About Us

Sunflower Park Estate is a thoughtfully master-planned mixed-use community located behind T.A Gardens Estate along the Lagos–Ibadan Expressway. Less than 25 minutes from Alausa, Ikeja, the estate combines modern infrastructure with generous green spaces to deliver secure, healthy, and valuable living.

Designed around family living and long-term value, Sunflower Park provides a range of residential and commercial options, well-lit road networks, sustainable drainage, and recreational amenities. Our approach balances convenience with nature so residents enjoy both accessibility and a peaceful environment.

Highlights: 60-acre master plan, residential & commercial plots, eco-friendly landscaping, gated security, and community-focused amenities that support lasting investment returns.

Our Vision

To make home ownership an achievable reality for as many people as possible by delivering well-planned communities that balance convenience, safety and green living.

Our Mission

To discover and present valuable land opportunities, guide buyers through practical financing options, and build sustainable neighbourhoods that deliver long-term value.
